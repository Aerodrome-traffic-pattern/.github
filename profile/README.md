# Aerodrome - Cross-Platform Flight Planning Workspace

> **A calm cockpit companion for traffic patterns, runway layouts, weather briefings, and navigation logs.**

## Why This Exists

Flight planning often scatters across paper notes, separate weather pages, and half-remembered radio calls. **Aerodrome** brings traffic pattern review, runway layout study, weather briefing checks, and navigation log keeping into one readable workspace. Instead of guessing which leg needs attention, pilots open Aerodrome, review the route, and move from weather to runway to log without losing context.

Aerodrome solves **one specific problem**: disconnected pre-flight information. Just open Aerodrome, choose a route or airfield, and see Aerodrome traffic pattern notes, Aerodrome flight planning fields, and a practical Aerodrome navigation log together. If you are tired of juggling tabs and scribbled notes before departure, **Aerodrome** is for you.
